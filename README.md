# Riverside-power-distribution-operations-analytics
Riverside Power Distribution Company (RPDC): Operational Reliability &amp; Customer Impact Analytics 

## Executive Summary: This repository contains a comprehensive Business &amp; Data Analytics project focused on evaluating the operational performance and reliability of the **Riverside Power Distribution Company (RPDC)** grid infrastructure.   

The project delivers an end-to-end data pipeline from raw data ingestion, transformation, and root-cause diagnostic queries to an executive-level interactive Power BI dashboard designed to optimize grid reliability and mitigate customer impact.


##  Strategic Objectives
* **Operational Reliability Metrics:** Evaluate system outage durations, service interruptions, and downtime trends across distribution networks.
* **Root Cause & Diagnostic Analysis:** Pinpoint high-frequency failure drivers across equipment, environmental conditions, and maintenance vulnerabilities.
* **Customer Impact & Risk Mapping:** Quantify service disruption severity across residential, commercial, and industrial service zones.
* **Executive Decision Support:** Deliver intuitive visual reporting to support operational leadership in resource allocation and outage mitigation strategies.
